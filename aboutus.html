About AstraSync

We are a smart wearable technology company that believes health technology should look as good as it works.

Our story

AstraSync began with a simple frustration. Our founders wore fitness trackers that gave them useful numbers, but the devices looked bulky and ended up in a drawer after a few weeks.

They asked a question: what if a health device looked like something you already love to wear? That question became AstraSync, a company that treats design and technology as equal partners.

Today every AstraSync device connects to the AstraSync app and cloud platform, turning daily activity into clear, personal insights.

Our mission

To create smart wearable devices that people enjoy wearing every day, combining comfortable design with meaningful health, fitness and lifestyle insights.

Our vision

A world where technology fades into the background of daily life and helps everyone feel more confident, comfortable and in control of their wellbeing.

Our commitment to innovation

We keep improving how our devices sense, connect and fit. Each new product goes through comfort testing, battery testing and app testing before it reaches customers.

Our logo

The AstraSync logo is a bold letter A with a curved plum swoosh sweeping around it.

The letter A stands for "Astra", the Latin word for stars, and its rising peak represents progress and performance. The swoosh stands for "Sync", the constant flow of data between your wearable, your phone and the cloud.

Black and deep plum were chosen because they feel premium, modern and calm, which matches our mix of technology and style.
